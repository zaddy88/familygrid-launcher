# FamilyGrid Launcher – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 7 November 2026_

FamilyGrid Launcher ("the app") is a home-screen app for Android made by Zaka Ul Haque.

## The short version

**The app collects nothing, sends nothing, and has no accounts, ads or analytics.**
Everything you set up stays on your phone.

## What the app stores on your phone

- Your tile layout: which apps are shown, their names and colours.
- People you add as speed-dial tiles: the **name and phone number you choose**.
- Settings: language, text size, tiles per row, what to show, and an optional PIN
  (stored only as a one-way hash, never as the PIN itself).

This data is kept in the app's private storage. It is deleted if you uninstall the app or
clear its data. It is never uploaded anywhere.

## Permissions and why

| Permission | Used for |
|---|---|
| Network state | Showing whether Wi-Fi and the internet are working. No network requests are made. |
| Call phone (optional) | Letting a person tile call straight away. |
| Bluetooth / Bluetooth connect (optional) | Showing the name of a connected headset, speaker or car. |
| Contacts (only through the system contact picker) | Reading the **one** contact you pick, to save its name and number on a tile. The app can't see your other contacts. |

## Accessibility service ("FamilyGrid big buttons")

If you switch it on, this service draws large Back, Home and Apps buttons and performs only
the Back and Home actions when you tap them. It is set up so it **cannot read screen content**
(`canRetrieveWindowContent="false"`). It does not collect, store or share any data.

## Children

The app is meant to be set up by adults for older relatives and is not directed at children.
